for FLC students

New since June 1, 2026: if you are enrolled at least half-time (usually 6 units) in an associate's or bachelor's degree program at FLC, CalFresh's student rule no longer blocks you.

Before today, had you heard about this change?

$306
per month, max One person · from Oct 1, 2026

CalFresh gives up to $306 a month for groceries for a single person. At Folsom prices, that buys a 14-item basket of staples like milk, eggs, chicken, and bread 5.3 times a month (Cart Index, week of Sep 21, 2026).

*** INFO, NOT A DECISION ***

Who may qualify

The student rule is one test. Income and household rules still apply, and the county makes the final decision.

Enrolled at least half-time

You are enrolled at least half-time (usually 6 units) in an associate's or bachelor's degree program.

Under the income limit

Your income is under the CalFresh limit. GetCalFresh lists the current limits.

Under 22 and living with a parent?

You apply with that parent's household.

CalFresh does not affect your financial aid.
