Who may qualify

The student rule is one test. Income and household rules still apply, and the county makes the final decision.

Enrolled at least half-time

You are enrolled at least half-time (usually 6 units) in an associate's or bachelor's degree program at a California community college, CSU, or UC.

Under the income limit

Your income is under the CalFresh limit. GetCalFresh lists the current limits.

Under 22 and living with a parent?

You apply with that parent's household.

CalFresh does not affect your financial aid. Official student rules: California Department of Social Services, CalFresh for college students.
